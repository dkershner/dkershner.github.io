Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Lightning Meal Plans. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Encrypted authentication credentials
  • Account preferences

2.2 Dietary and Meal Information

To provide personalized meal planning services, we collect:

  • Dietary preferences and restrictions
  • Food allergies and intolerances
  • Meal history and ratings
  • Recipe customizations
  • Shopping lists
  • Weekly meal plans

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Generating personalized meal plans and AI recommendations
  • Organizing shopping lists by department
  • Managing account access and subscription status
  • Improving app functionality and user experience
  • Sending critical service notifications

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit and at rest, to protect your personal data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy:

  • Account Information: Retained while your account is active and for up to 30 days after account deletion to allow for account recovery.
  • Meal Plans and Recipes: Retained while your account is active to provide continuous service and personalization.
  • Shopping Lists: Retained while your account is active, allowing you to access and manage your lists.
  • Dietary Preferences: Retained while your account is active to ensure accurate meal recommendations.
  • Usage Analytics: Aggregated, anonymized data may be retained indefinitely for service improvement purposes.

After you delete your account, all personal data is permanently removed from our active systems within 30 days. Some information may remain in backup systems for up to 90 days as part of our disaster recovery procedures, after which it is permanently deleted.

6. Data Deletion and Your Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data at any time. We provide multiple ways to delete your data:

6.1 In-App Account Deletion

You can delete your account directly from the Lightning Meal Plans app:

  1. Open the Lightning Meal Plans app
  2. Navigate to Profile
  3. Select "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm your deletion request

This will immediately remove your account access and schedule permanent deletion of all your personal data within 30 days.

6.2 Email Deletion Request

You can also request account and data deletion by emailing us at support@lightningmealplans.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Please include:

  • The email address associated with your account
  • Confirmation that you want to permanently delete your account and all associated data

We will process your request within 7 business days and send you a confirmation email once your data has been scheduled for deletion.

6.3 What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following information is permanently removed:

  • Your account credentials and email address
  • All saved meal plans and schedules
  • Your recipe collection and customizations
  • Shopping lists and grocery history
  • Dietary preferences and restrictions
  • Meal ratings and notes
  • Any other personal information associated with your account

6.4 Additional Rights

In addition to deletion, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data by emailing support@lightningmealplans.com
  • Correction: Update or correct your information directly in the app settings
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format for transfer to another service

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, data retention, or deletion procedures, please contact us at support@lightningmealplans.com.
