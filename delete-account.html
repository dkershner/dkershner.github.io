Account Deletion Overview

Last updated: January 12, 2026

We understand that you may want to delete your Lightning Meal Plans account. This page explains how to request account deletion and what happens to your data when you do.

How to Delete Your Account In-App

To delete your Lightning Meal Plans account directly from your mobile device:

  1. Open the Lightning Meal Plans app on your mobile device
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab (located in the bottom navigation bar)
  3. Scroll down and select "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm your decision when prompted

⚠️ Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Please make sure you want to proceed.

Alternative: Email Deletion Request

If you are unable to access the app, you can submit a manual account deletion request by emailing our team:

Email: support@lightningmealplans.com
Subject line: Account Deletion Request

Please send the email from the email address associated with your Lightning Meal Plans account so we can verify ownership.

Data Retention & Deletion Details

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently erased within 30 days:

  • Your user account profile (email address, display name, preferences)
  • All custom recipes, imported recipes, and meal plans
  • Your saved grocery lists and meal history ratings
