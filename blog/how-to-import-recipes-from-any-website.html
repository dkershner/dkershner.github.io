Import Any Recipe with One Click: Stop Copy-Pasting Ingredients

Written by Lightning Meal Plans Team • Published July 28, 2026
🌐

We all have favorite food blogs, social media posts, and recipe sites saved in browser bookmarks. But trying to manually copy ingredients into a grocery list while ignoring endless ad popups and long life stories is frustrating.

The One-Click Solution

With Lightning Meal Plans, importing a web recipe is as effortless as copying a link. Our backend extractor parses HTML structures and structured schema metadata to pull only what matters:

  • Recipe Title & Cover Image
  • Parsed Ingredients & Quantities
  • Clear Step-by-Step Instructions
  • Original Serving Sizes (which you can scale up or down with a single tap)

✨ Pro Tip: Once a recipe is imported, it lives permanently in your personal recipe library. Even if the website changes or goes down, your saved recipe stays safe and accessible in the app!

Step-by-Step: How to Import a Recipe

  1. Copy the URL: Find any recipe link on your browser or mobile phone and copy it to your clipboard.
  2. Open Lightning Meal Plans: Tap the Import Recipe button in the app.
  3. Paste & Extract: Paste the link. Within seconds, the AI parses the ingredients and adds the meal to your plan!

Automatic Scaling & Smart Grocery List Integration

Importing a recipe is only half the magic. Once imported, you can change the serving size from 2 to 6, and Lightning Meal Plans will recalculate every ingredient ratio automatically before sending it straight to your departmental grocery list.

Say goodbye to clutter and manual notes. Download Lightning Meal Plans today and centralize your entire recipe collection!
