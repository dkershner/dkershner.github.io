Frequently Asked Questions

How does recipe import work?

Simply paste a URL from your favorite recipe website. Our AI analyzes the page and extracts the ingredients, quantities, and cooking instructions for you.

Is the shopping list smart?

Yes! It doesn't just list items; it organizes them by department (Produce, Meat, Dairy, etc.) and combines duplicates so you know exactly what to buy.

Can I exclude ingredients I dislike?

Absolutely. You can set permanent dislikes and allergies in your profile. The AI will avoid suggesting meals with these ingredients.

Is the app really completely free?

Yes! Lightning Meal Plans is 100% free to use. There are no paid plans, subscriptions, or hidden "premium" features. Enjoy full access to AI meal planning and recipe imports at no cost.

Do the AI meal suggestions use real recipes?

Yes! A major difference between Lightning Meal Plans and other AI apps is that our AI searches the web for real, tried-and-true recipes. It never "hallucinates" or invents untested meals, meaning you always get legitimate recipes that you can cook with confidence.

What platforms are supported?

Lightning Meal Plans is currently available for Android devices on Google Play. An iOS version is in development and coming soon.
