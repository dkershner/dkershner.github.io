Frequently Asked Questions

How do I import a recipe from a website?

In the app, tap the "Import Recipe" button and paste the URL from any recipe website. Our AI will automatically extract the ingredients and instructions. If you encounter issues with a specific site, please contact support with the URL.

How do I set my dietary preferences?

Go to your Profile or Settings in the app, then select "Dietary Preferences." You can choose from Keto, Vegan, Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, and more. You can also add specific allergies and dislikes.

How does the shopping list work?

When you add meals to your plan, ingredients are automatically added to your shopping list. The list is organized by department (Produce, Dairy, Meat, etc.) and duplicate items are merged with combined quantities.

Can I adjust serving sizes?

Yes! When viewing a meal, you can adjust the number of servings. All ingredient quantities will automatically scale to match your selection.

How do AI meal suggestions work?

Our AI searches for real, tested recipes from the web based on your dietary preferences, past ratings, and restrictions. Unlike other apps, we only suggest legitimate recipes that have been published online, never invented combinations.

How do I manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your app store account (Google Play or Apple App Store). To cancel or modify your subscription, open your device's app store, go to your account settings, and select "Subscriptions."

What's included in the free version?

You can download and use Lightning Meal Plans for free to explore the features. A subscription unlocks unlimited AI meal suggestions, unlimited recipe imports, and full access to smart shopping lists and meal planning tools.

I'm having technical issues. What should I do?

First, try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, make sure you have the latest version installed. If problems continue, email us at support@lightningmealplans.com with details about the issue and your device model.

How do I delete my account?

To delete your account and all associated data, please visit our account deletion page or contact us at support@lightningmealplans.com with your request.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes. We take your privacy seriously. Your meal plans, recipes, and personal information are stored securely and never shared with third parties. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

What recipe websites are supported for import?

Our AI can import from most popular recipe websites. If you find a site that doesn't work, please send us the URL at support@lightningmealplans.com and we'll work to add support for it.
