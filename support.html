Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Find fast answers to common questions about recipe importing, shopping lists, and subscriptions.

In the app, tap the "Import Recipe" button and paste the URL from any recipe website. Our AI will automatically extract the ingredients, quantities, and cooking instructions. If you encounter issues with a specific site, email support with the recipe URL and our engineers will investigate.
Go to your Profile or Settings tab in the app, then select "Dietary Preferences." You can select standard diets (Keto, Vegan, Vegetarian, Gluten-Free) and list specific food dislikes or allergies. The app strictly filters future AI recommendations based on your preferences.
When you add meals to your weekly calendar, ingredients are automatically added to your shopping list. The list is organized by supermarket department (Produce, Dairy, Meat, Pantry) and matching ingredients across multiple recipes are automatically merged with combined total quantities.
Yes! When viewing any meal or recipe, tap the serving size calculator to adjust the number of servings up or down. All ingredient measurements will scale automatically before being added to your grocery list.
Our AI searches real, tested recipes from trusted food websites based on your dietary rules, past meal ratings, and disliked ingredients. Unlike other tools that generate weird AI ingredient combinations, we only recommend tried-and-true published recipes.
Subscriptions are managed through your mobile device app store account (Google Play Store on Android or Apple App Store on iOS). Open your device app store, tap your account profile icon, select "Subscriptions," and choose Lightning Meal Plans to modify or cancel.
You can download Lightning Meal Plans for free to explore features and test meal planning. A subscription unlocks unlimited AI meal suggestions, unlimited web recipe imports, and full access to smart shopping lists and meal prep tools.
